Eko Kitchen Menu

Authentic Yoruba flavors right in the heart of Lagos.

Appetizers

  • Asun (Spicy Peppered Goat Meat)

    Delectable bite-sized pieces of goat meat, grilled and sautéed in hot habanero peppers and onions.

  • Akara (Bean Cakes)

    Golden, crispy deep-fried bean cakes made from peeled black-eyed peas, onions, and fresh spices.

  • Moin Moin (Steamed Bean Pudding)

    Rich, savory steamed bean pudding blended with peppers, onions, and enriched with boiled eggs.

Main Courses

  • Amala with Ewedu & Gbegiri

    Traditional soft yam flour swallow served with smooth jute leaf soup, rich local bean soup, and spicy pepper sauce (Obe Ata).

  • Eba with Efo Riro

    Pan-fried cassava flakes dough paired with a rich, aromatic rich spinach stew cooked with locust beans, stockfish, and assorted meats.

  • Smokey Lagos Jeylof Rice

    Iconic party-style long grain rice cooked in a savory, smoky tomato and bell pepper broth, served with fried plantains (Dodo).

Desserts

  • Sweet Fried Plantain (Dodo Cube Sweetener)

    Perfectly caramelized, over-ripe sweet plantain cubes dusted with a pinch of cinnamon sugar.

  • Puff Puff with Honey Drizzle

    Sweet, fluffy, and spongy deep-fried dough balls served warm with a local honey glaze.

  • Chilled Kunu Zaki Granita

    A refreshing, iced-crushed twist on the traditional spiced millet drink, sweetened with sweet potato and ginger notes.
